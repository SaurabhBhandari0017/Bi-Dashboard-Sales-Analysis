# Super Store Sales Analysis & Forecasting 📊

## Objective
### Analyze Super Store sales data (2019–2020) to uncover trends, customer behavior, and provide a short-term forecast that supports better business decisions.

## Description
### This project is built entirely in **Power BI**. It delivers interactive dashboards covering sales, profit, customer segments, payment modes, shipping preferences, and regional performance. A **15-day forecasting model** is included, along with actionable recommendations for business growth.

## Features
### - 📈 Year-over-Year Sales & Profit Trends  
### - 🌍 Regional & State-wise Performance  
### - 💳 Payment & Shipping Mode Analysis  
### - 🛒 Category & Sub-Category Insights  
### - 🔮 15-Day Forecasting Model  
### - ✅ Business Recommendations  

## Key Insights
### - December is the peak sales month → focus festive offers.  
### - COD dominates (43%) → encourage online payments with discounts.  
### - California & New York lead sales → target with loyalty programs.  
### - Standard shipping preferred → optimize free delivery schemes.
